Introductory machine learning and deep learning lectures with hands-on practice.
Includes a visual presentation of artificial neural networks created in [Manim](https://www.manim.community/) in collaboration with [Ziad Kheil](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ziad-kheil-175279168/) (doing remarkable work). The animation is available [here](https://lucas-rbnt.github.io/deep_learning_manim/) | 2022 - 2025



Machine Learning Foundations
======
* Core supervised models - linear/logistic regression, SVM, trees, ensembles
* Unsupervised techniques - clustering, dimensionality reduction
* Model evaluation, validation, metrics


Optimization and Training Principles
======
- Loss functions and gradient-based optimization
- Overfitting, underfitting, and generalization
- Practical training workflows and debugging

Deep Learning
======
- Fully Connected Neural networks
- Backpropagation principles
- Convolutional Neural Networks
